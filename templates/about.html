About {{ site_name }}

{{ site_name }} (Urdu for “pen”) turns typed text into pages that look handwritten. You can use one of our handwriting styles, upload any font, or make a font from your own handwriting with a printed template and a photo.

What people use it for

  • Notes, study sheets and flashcards that are easier to read than a messy scrawl
  • Cards, letters and invitations with a personal touch
  • Journals, scrapbooks, social media posts and other creative work
  • Writing by hand when it’s painful or slow, for example because of an injury or a condition like dysgraphia

Please use it honestly

If a teacher, exam or form asks for your own handwriting, they mean writing done by your hand. Don’t hand in a generated page in its place, and don’t use someone else’s handwriting font to pass off writing as theirs.

Who makes it

{{ site_name }} is built by an independent developer in Karachi. Questions, ideas and bug reports are welcome on the contact page.
