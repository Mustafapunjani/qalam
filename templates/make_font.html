Make a font from your handwriting

It takes one printout, a pen and your phone camera. Your photos are used only to build the font and aren’t kept.

  1. Print the template

    Two A4 pages. Print at 100% size, not “fit to page”.

    Download template (PDF)

  2. Fill it in

    Use a dark pen and write one character in each box, sitting on the solid line. Small letters are written three times and capitals twice, so your text won’t repeat the exact same shape.

  3. Upload a photo or scan of each page

Your font is ready
